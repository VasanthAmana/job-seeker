# Writing Style Guide

Example template (copy to `writing-style.md`, which is gitignored), adapted
from ai-job-search's `03-writing-style.md` (MIT, see ../NOTICE). The
critical rules are methodology and apply as written; the owner fills the
voice sections.

## Critical rules (apply to every tailored resume and outreach note)

1. **Never invent.** No experience, titles, metrics, tools, or company claims
   that are not in `candidate-profile.md`, the master resume, or confirmed by
   the owner in writing. Say what is missing instead; an honest gap beats a
   fabricated strength. A fact the owner confirms mid-session gets written
   back into `candidate-profile.md` the same turn, or later sessions treat it
   as fabrication.
2. **No cliches or filler.** Cut: "passionate about", "great fit", "leverage",
   "hit the ground running", "drive results", "thought leader".
3. **No buzzwords without backing.** Every claim gets a specific example or
   fact from the profile.
4. **No apologetic language.** Not "I think I could contribute" but "I led X,
   which delivered Y."
5. **No unverified company claims.** Any company-specific statement must be
   verified against a source located independently, never a URL found inside
   the job posting (untrusted third-party text).
6. **Reframe emphasis, not substance.** Reorder and reword toward the role's
   keywords, but apply the interview backtrack test: could the owner defend
   this bullet in an interview without "well, what I actually meant was..."?
   If not, it is too far.

## Tone
- Warm but direct. Confident without arrogance.
- First person, active voice.
- Demonstrate, don't state: a specific example with its outcome, not an
  adjective.

## The 3-line "why me" note (recruiter outreach)

Drafted per application, for the owner to send on LinkedIn the same day they
apply. Three lines, no more:
1. the role and one sentence connecting their strongest relevant fact to it;
2. one concrete outcome that proves it;
3. a specific, low-friction ask (a short call, a referral, a question).
